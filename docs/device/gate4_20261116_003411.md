### The accounting trap (do not quote `smaps` for a GPU process)

For the same PID the two sources disagree by **2.93×**: `smaps_rollup` says 85,137 kB, `dumpsys meminfo` says 249,290 kB. The difference is the **GL mtrack** row (164,192 kB) — the Mali driver's own accounting of the CL buffers this process owns, which never appears in `/proc/<pid>/smaps`. Gate 4 therefore uses the `dumpsys` TOTAL PSS (the larger, conservative number); the smaps figure is recorded only as the cross-check.

## What this proves / does not

- proves: both stages ran concurrently over ONE artifact file; the GPU stage holds exactly one `MAP_SHARED` view of it, at offset 0, sized exactly as the file, and its 10/10 weight windows are imported by reference rather than copied; the NPU stage opens only that one path, hands it to NNAPI, and never maps the artifact itself; and the combined PSS of the two stages is far under the 2.2 GiB budget
